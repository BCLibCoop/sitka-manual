Viewing Transit Details
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
(((Transit Details)))

Transit dates, source, and destination can be viewed through Item Status.

. Go to *Circulation -> Item Status* and scan the item barcode, or click on item barcode 
hyperlink to load the item in the _Item Status_ screen.
. Click *Detail View*.
+
image:images/circ/transit-detail-1.png[scaledwidth="75%"]
+
. Select the _Holds/Transit_ tab.
+
image:images/circ/transit-detail-2.png[scaledwidth="75%"]
+
Transit information for the most recent transit is displayed on the right side of the screen.
+
image:images/circ/transit-detail-3.png[scaledwidth="75%"]
