Cancelling Transits
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
(((Cancel Transit)))

A transit can be cancelled when an item is being erroneously sent to another branch or library.

Whenever possible libraries should use the check in modifier _Suppress Holds and Transit_ to prevent
unwanted transits and give library staff time to update the item to preventit from triggering a transit
or resolve whatever needs to be resolved before it can go into transit.
 
[CAUTION]
=========
The option to cancel a transit should be used sparingly and only under some circumstances as it 
effectively cancels the previous check in. Cancelling a transit affects data integrity and the 
accuracy of circulation statistics.  

As per https://docs.libraries.coop/policy/_inter_library_loan.html[Interlibrary Connect policy], 
libraries participating in Interlibrary Connect should only cancel a transit if they own the item and 
they are unable to send the item to fill a hold at another library.
=========

Cancelling a Transit at Check In
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

. Select the item you wish to cancel the transit for and from choose *Cancel Transits* from the
*Actions* menu.
+
image:images/circ/cancel-transit-check-in-1.png[scaledwidth="75%"]
+
. On the pop-up that appears click *OK/Continue* to proceed with cancelling the transit.
. The item will now have the status of _Canceled Transit_. Resolve the reason why the item was
being sent into transit in the first place.

[NOTE]
======
If a hold as been cancelled after an item has already been sent into transit to fill the hold, Evergreen 
will continue to try and send the item to the pick up library. 

To resolve this, cancel the transit and then check in the item using the check in modifier _Suppress 
Holds and Transit_. The item's status will update to _Reshelving_ and should behave normally 
going forward.
======

Cancelling a Transit from the Transit List
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

. Go to *Administration -> Local Administration -> Transit List*.
. Check the radio button for *Transits From*.
+
image:images/circ/cancel-transit-list-1.png[scaledwidth="75%"]
+
. Adjust the start and end date as needed.
+
image:images/circ/cancel-transit-list-2.png[scaledwidth="75%"]
+
. Select the transit you would like to cancel and click *Cancel Transit*.
+
image:images/circ/cancel-transit-list-3.png[scaledwidth="75%"]
+
. The pop-up that appears will indicate if there are any associate holds. To proceed 
with cancelling the transit click *Cancel Transit*.
+
image:images/circ/cancel-transit-list-4.png[scaledwidth="75%"]
+
. The item's status is updated to _Canceled Transit_. Resolve the reason why the item was
being sent into transit in the first place.

[NOTE]
======
If a hold as been cancelled after an item has already been sent into transit to fill the hold, Evergreen 
will continue to try and send the item to the pick up library. 

To resolve this, cancel the transit and then check in the item using the check in modifier _Suppress 
Holds and Transit_. The item's status will update to _Reshelving_ and should behave normally 
going forward.
======
