Holdings Templates
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

link:https://youtu.be/evqF-tOekD4[*Sitka Snippet Video - Creating and Using Holdings Templates (2:21)*]

Holdings templates allow you to set up templates with pre-selected values for item attributes
and help to ensure consistency in cataloguing of items.

To access the Holdings Templates interface click on the _Holdings Templates_ tab in the Holdings Editor or go to 
*Administration -> Local Administration -> Holdings Template Editor*.

From the Holdings Templates interface you can view, create, edit, delete, import, and export templates.

image::images/cat/holdings/holdings-template-1.png[]

Creating Holdings Templates
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

. In the Holdings Templates, click *Create Template*. 
+
image::images/cat/holdings/holdings-template-create-1.png[]
+
. The Holdings Templates Editor will open in a new tab.
. Enter the values for the call number and item attributes needed for your template.
+
image::images/cat/holdings/holdings-template-create-2.png[]
+
. Enter a name for the template and click *Save as New Template*.
+
image::images/cat/holdings/holdings-template-create-3.png[]
+
. The template is saved. Close the tab to return to the Holdings Editor.

Editing Holdings Templates
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

. In the Holdings Templates, select the template you would like to edit and click *Edit Selected*. 
+
image::images/cat/holdings/holdings-template-edit-1.png[]
+
. The Holdings Templates Editor will open in a new tab.
. Make the desired changes and click Save Templare.
+
image::images/cat/holdings/holdings-template-edit-2.png[]
+
. The template is saved. Close the tab to return to the Holdings Editor.


Deleting Holdings Templates
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

. In the Holdings Templates, select the template you would like to delete and click *Delete Selected*. 
+
image::images/cat/holdings/holdings-template-delete-1.png[]
+
. A pop-up will appear. Click *OK* to confirm the deletion.
+
image::images/cat/holdings/holdings-template-delete-2.png[]
+
. The template is deleted and no longer appears in the grid.

Sharing Holdings Template
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

link:https://youtu.be/HPh3qi921Bw[*Sitka Snippet Video - Sharing Holdings Templates (2:13)*]

Templates are saved on the Evergreen server, but only viewable by the staff account that created them.  
To share templates with other staff account you need to export the templates and have
 staff import them into their accounts.
  
[TIP]
=====
When importing holdings templates, existing templates will be kept and new ones added. Templates sharing 
the same name will be overlaid by the new templates.
=====

. In the Holdings Templates, click *Export All* or select the specific template(s) you would like to export and 
click *Export Selected*. 
+
image::images/cat/holdings/holdings-template-share-1.png[]
+
. The file downloads to your computer.
+
. You can share the file with staff via email, via a shared network drive, or
have them log into Evergreen on the computer the file has been saved to.
. In the Holdings Templates, click *Import*.
+
image::images/cat/holdings/holdings-template-share-2.png[]
+
. Follow the prompts to locate the templates file to be loaded. Click *Open*.
. The templates will be imported and display in the grid.
