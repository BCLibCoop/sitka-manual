Transferring Holdings
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

There are times when you need to move item records from one title record to another, or move items
from one call number to another under the same or another title record. There are functions in
Evergreen to allow you to do so. The critical step is to ensure that you first mark the destination
record, then move the source record with the corresponding transfer function.

[TIP]
=====
For multi-branch libraries, if you wish to transfer holdings between branches this should be done by updating 
the item attributes for _Circulating Library_ and _Owning Library_. See xref:_the_holdings_editor[] for
more information.
=====


[[_transfer_holding]]
Transferring Holdings
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

When you want to transfer all items under one call number to another title record under the same title,
you use the *Transfer Holdings* function. Evergreen will transfer both
the call number record and all items under it to the new title.

. Find the record you would like to move your holdings to. 
. From the *Mark for* drop down menu, select *Holding Transfer*.
+
image::images/cat/transfer-holding-1.png[]
+
. A confirmation message reading _Holding Transfer Target Set_ will appear at bottom right of the screen.
+
[NOTE]
======
Once you set a transfer target it will be kept until a new target is marked or the record marks are reset
by going to *Mark for* -> *Reset Record Marks*.

image::images/cat/transfer-reset-record-marks-1.png[]
======
+
. Retrieve the record that the holdings you would like to transfer are currently attached to.
. Click the _Holdings View_ tab and select the call number you want to transfer. 
+
image::images/cat/transfer-holding-2.png[]
+
. From the *Actions* menu select *Transfer Holdings to Marked Destination*.
+
image::images/cat/transfer-holding-3.png[]
+
. The screen automatically refreshes and the selected holdings no longer appear in the grid.
. Manually refresh the record you transferred the holdings to and confirm they now display there as expected.


[TIP]
========
If the call number for the holdings being transferred matches exactly to an existing call number for your library
on the record it is being transferred to Evergreen will merge the call numbers together and you will see all
items under the one call number.
========

[[_transfer_holdings_another_branch]]
Transferring Holdings to Another Branch
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++


When you want to transfer all items under one call number to another
library under the same title, you use the *Transfer Holdings* function. Evergreen will transfer both
the call number record and all items under it to the new library.

. Find the destination or target record. On *Mark for* dropdown list, select *Holding Transfer*.
+
image::images/cat/transfer-holding-1.png[]
+
. Confirmation appears at bottom right of the screen.
+
image::images/cat/transfer-holding-2.png[]
+
NOTE: Transfer target is kept until it is reset (via *Reset Record Marks* on *Mark for* list) or a new target is marked.
+
. Go to *Holdings View* of the source title, and select the destination library.
.. You may need to select *Show Empty Libs* to see your destination.
+
image::images/cat/transfer-holding-5.png[]
+
Click *Actions* dropdown list. Scroll down to *Mark*. Select *Mark Library/Call Number as Transfer Destination*.
+
image::images/cat/transfer-holding-6.png[]
+
. Confirmation appears at the bottom right corner of the screen.
+
. Find the source title. Select the source volume on *Holdings View*.
+
image::images/cat/transfer-holding-7.png[]
+
. Click *Actions* dropdown list. Scroll down to *Transfer*. Select
*Transfer Holdings to Marked Destination*.
+
image::images/cat/transfer-holding-3.png[]
+
. Confirmation appears at bottom right of the screen.


[TIP]
=======
If the destination library has a call number with the same call number, the call numbers will be merged during the transfer.
=======

[[_transfer_item]]
Transferring Items
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

You may want to transfer some of the items under one call number to another call number or transfer all
items under one call number to another with a different call number. You use the  *Transfer Items* function.

. Find the destination title record.

. On *Holdings View*, select the destination call number/library.

. Click *Actions* dropdown. Go to *Mark* to select *Library/Call Number as Transfer Destination*.
+
image::images/cat/transfer-holding-4.png[]
+
. Go to the source record, if different from the destination.
. On *Holdings View*, select the items you wish to transfer.
. Click *Actions* dropdown list. Go to *Transfer* to select *Transfer Items to Marked Destination*.
+
image::images/cat/transfer-holding-5.png[]
+
. The source item disappears, if destination is under another record.
. The confirmation pops up at the bottom
right of the screen.
+
image::images/cat/transfer-holding-6.png[]

[NOTE]
======
You can xref:_working_with_items_in_a_bucket[use an item bucket] to transfer a number of items at once.

After the destination or target record has been marked as the target, navigate to the item bucket with items to be transferred. Then, select the items in the bucket that should be transferred, and from the *Actions* menu select *Transfer Selected Items to Marked Call Number*.
======
