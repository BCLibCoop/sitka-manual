Removing Records from the Basket
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

. Uncheck the check box next to the record. This can be done from the search results page or 
from xref:_staff_cat_view_basket[within the basket].
+
image::images/staffcat/basket/staff-cat-basket-remove-records-1.png[]
+
. The record is removed from the basket and the count beside the basket icon will decrease.
+
image::images/staffcat/basket/staff-cat-basket-remove-records-2.png[]

[TIP]
=====
You can also remove all records from the basket at once by clearing the basket. See xref:_staff_cat_basket_clear_basket[].
=====
