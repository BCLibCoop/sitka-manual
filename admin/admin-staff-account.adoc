Library Staff Accounts
----------------------

In Evergreen library staff accounts are the same as patron accounts with two differences.

. Library staff accounts use Main (Profile) Permissions Groups with permissions that give access to 
Evergreen’s staff functions.
. Library staff accounts have working locations which tell Evergreen which libraries or branches 
an account can be used at.

[NOTE]
======
When a library staff account expires the staff member is not able to log into the 
Evergreen staff client until their account has been renewed. 

Local system administrators can renew all library staff accounts except local system administrators. 
https://bc.libraries.coop/support/[Contact Co-op Support] to have a local system administrator account renewed.
======

There are two kinds of library staff accounts recommended by Co-op Support:

*Library Staff Specific Accounts*

* Account is specific to a particular staff member and includes their personal details like first 
and last name.
* Account is used for staff functions only, never used for personal borrowing.
* Account can be granted permissions to use the reporter and/or view report output.
* Account can be updated and transferred to a new staff member preserving access to recurring reports
that run from the reporter.  The new user MUST complete a 
 https://bc.libraries.coop/support/sitka/reporter-privacy-waiver/[reporter privacy waiver] to comply 
 with Sitka’s privacy policy.
 
[NOTE]
======
Libraries are encouraged to use Library Staff Specific Accounts for all accounts that need access to the 
reporter in Evergreen to avoid the need to re-set up recurring reports.
======

*Generic Library Staff Accounts*

* Account is generally specific to a department or function, such as circulation or cataloguing, and is used
by multiple staff members.
* Account name is not tied to a specific person.  For example, Circulation FrontDesk is a commonly used name.
* Account CANNOT be granted permissions to use the reporter.


*Library Staff/Personal Accounts - No Longer Recommended*

* Account is specific to a particular staff member and includes their personal details like first 
and last name.
* Account is used both for performing staff functions and for personal borrowing.
* Account can be granted permissions to use the reporter and/or view report output.
* Account CANNOT be transferred to a new staff member as it is linked to personal borrowing data.

[NOTE]
======
Co-op Support now strongly recommends against using Library Staff/Personal Accounts due to issues that
have arisen when libraries are closing down a staff member's account after they leave the library.
======



Creating Library Staff Accounts
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

link:https://youtu.be/8GXNpN-pxiM[*Sitka Training Video - Creating Library Staff Accounts (4:46)*]

To create a new library staff account register your user as you would a patron taking the following 
into consideration:

* It is best practice to use a temporary password and then have your staff reset their password to something 
only they know.
* For an account to be a library staff account it needs to have a Main (Profile) Permission Group with library 
staff permissions.  Choose the appropriate permission group for the access the staff member needs.  

[NOTE]
======
The Local System Administrator permission group can only be assigned by Co-op Support.  Libraries may create 
an account using any permission group and then https://bc.libraries.coop/support/[contact Co-op Support] to 
have the account updated to a local system administrator. Please include the account's barcode in the request.
======

The table in 
http://docs.libraries.coop/sitka/_staff_account_permission_groups.html[Staff Account Permission Groups]
 shows what staff functions different permission groups can perform.

Working Locations
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
[[working-locations]]

link:https://youtu.be/VnY9NjaTxFc[*Sitka Snippet Video - Working Locations (1:05)*]

After creating the account you need to assign a working location:

. Retrieve the account.
. Go to *Other -> User Permission Editor*.
. Select the checkbox(es) in front of the library/branches that you want to assign to the account.
. Scroll down to the bottom to click *Save*.

image::images/admin/staff-account-3.png[]

=========
Library staff accounts without a working location cannot sign into the Evergreen staff client.

Editing Library Staff Accounts
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

link:https://youtu.be/kQ9HD5SFcFU[*Sitka Training Video - Editing Library Staff Accounts (1:08)*]


Editing Library Staff Accounts via My Account
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

All library staff can update their own username and password by logging 
into xref:_my_account[_My Account_] in the public catalogue.  Library staff can also
update their email address this way unless their libary has disabled the editing of email addresses
through My Account.

See xref:_resetting_library_staff_account_passwords[] for more ways a library staff password can be reset.

[CAUTION]
=========
Updating your library staff account in the public catalogue is the same as updating it in the staff client.

If you change your username and/or password in _My Account_ that is the username and/or password
you must now use to log into the staff client.
=========


Editing Library Staff Accounts via the Staff Client
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Local system administrators can edit all library staff accounts in the staff client the same as you 
would edit a patron, with the exception of local system administrator accounts. 

See xref:_resetting_library_staff_account_passwords[] for ways a library staff password can be reset.

Local system administrator accounts can only be edited in the staff client by Co-op Support. If a local 
system administrator account needs edits beyond what can be done via _My Account_ please
https://bc.libraries.coop/support/[contact Co-op Support] and include the account barcode and the changes needed.


Resetting Library Staff Account Passwords
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

https://youtu.be/YoB-JG6Y9Sk[*Sitka Training Video - Managing Passwords(4:02)*]


Library staff passwords can be reset:

* through My Account in your public catalogue.
** Log into _My Account_ and using the the *Change* button found on the _Personal Information_ tab of 
_Account Preferences_.
+
https://youtu.be/LprDPIhqN1I[*Sitka Snippet Video - Changing an Existing Password (1:20)*]
+
* through the password reset link in your public catalogue.
** Use the *Forgot Your Password?* link in the _My Account_ login screen on your public catalogue. You must have an email 
address recorded in your library staff account for this to method to work.
+
https://youtu.be/iJo5ajf-XAA[*Sitka Snippet Video - Resetting a Forgotten Password (2:54)*]
+
* through your account in the staff client by your local system administrator.
** Local system administrators can use the *Send Password Reset Link* button on the _Edit_ tab in the 
patron account in the staff client OR assign a new password to library staff that are not also local 
system administrators.

* by https://bc.libraries.coop/support/[contacting Co-op Support].
** If you are unable to reset your password using one of the other methods, send in a request to 
Co-op Support to have a temporary password assigned and then use the *Change* button in _My Account_.

[NOTE]
======
Resetting a password by email is disabled for libraries using single sign-on.  If your library uses
single sign-on you will not be able to use the *Forgot Your Password?* link in your public catalogue or the *Send Password Reset Link* button
in the staff client.

Please 
https://bc.libraries.coop/support/[contact Co-op Support] for the password reset URL for your library.
======



Staff Account Permission Groups
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Every library staff account must have a staff permission group assigned as their Main (Profile)
Permission Group in order to access the staff client.  Staff can also have secondary permission
groups assigned to give them access to special features.

Library directors and administrators should consider what functions each staff member needs access to and 
assign the appropriate permission group.

Secondary Permission Groups
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

These secondary permission permission groups can be granted to appropriate library staff accounts to give
access to specific features and functions that are not available through the main staff permission groups.

Acquisitions Supplementary Perm Groups
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

These secondary permission groups give library staff access to functions in the 
acquisitions module.  More information on these groups can be found in the 
https://docs.libraries.coop/acquisitions/_permissions.html#_acquisitions_permission_groups[Acquisitions Permission Groups section] of 
https://docs.libraries.coop/acquisitions/[Sitka's Evergreen Acquisitions Documentation].


Carousel Secondary Perms
++++++++++++++++++++++++

This secondary permission group gives library staff access to the carousel feature in Evergreen
that is used by LibPress libraries.

Library staff must https://bc.libraries.coop/support/[contact Co-op Support] if they need to have this
permission group added to their account.

See the https://help.libraries.coop/libpress/highlights/sitka-carousels/[Sitka Carousels section]
in the https://help.libraries.coop/libpress[LibPress Manual] for information on creating
and working with carousels.


Cover Image Upload Secondary Perms
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

This secondary permission group gives library staff access to upload custom cover images for bibliographic records through
the cover image uploader found in Evergreen's staff catalogue.

See xref:_uploading_cover_images[] for information on using this feature.

Local system administrators can grant this permission group to library staff accounts. 
See xref:_managing_secondary_permission_groups[] for instructions.

Reporter Full Permission
++++++++++++++++++++++++

This secondary permission group gives library staff access to the reporter in Evergreen to
run and view reports.

In accordance with Sitka’s privacy policy and owing to the nature of Sitka’s shared 
Evergreen database any staff needing access to the reporter must sign and submit the 
https://bc.libraries.coop/support/sitka/reporter-privacy-waiver/full-reporter-privacy-waiver/[Full 
Reporter Privacy Waiver].  Once the waiver is completed, if the staff account meets the 
https://bc.libraries.coop/support/sitka/reporter-privacy-waiver/[required criteria],
Co-op Support will grant reporter permissions to the account. 


Reporter View Output Permissions
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

This secondary permission group gives library staff access view report output that is shared with them 
via email or a link.

Local system administrators can grant this permission group to library staff accounts. See 
xref:_managing_secondary_permission_groups[] for instructions.

Additional permissions can be granted to library staff in two ways.


Managing Secondary Permission Groups
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

* xref:_granting_secondary_permission_group[Granting Secondary Permission Groups]
* xref:_removing_secondary_permission_group[Removing Secondary Permission Groups]

Granting Individual Permissions
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Local System Administrators may selectively grant additional permissions available to local system administrator 
accounts to other non-local system administrator accounts.

. Log in to Evergreen with a local system administrator account.
. Find the staff account in the *User Permission Editor*. You may search for the patron account, then go to 
*Others -> User Permission Editor*, or go to *Administration -> User Permission Editor* and search for 
the account by barcode.
. When the User Permission Editor is loaded, scroll down the permission list to find the desired permission. 
. Select the checkbox in *Applied* column. 
. Scroll down to the bottom to click *Save*.
+
image::images/admin/staff-account-2.png[]

[TIP]
=====
* *Permission*: List of permission names. For help correlating permissions to specific Evergreen functions 
please https://bc.libraries.coop/support/[contact Co-op Support].
* *Applied*: If checked the permission is granted to this account.
* *Depth*:  limits application to the staff member's library and should be left at the default. Evergreen 
blocks attempts to set Federation- or Sitka-wide privileges.
* *Grantable*: If checked this staff account will be able to grant the new privilege to other accounts 
(not recommended).
=====

Searching for Library Staff Accounts
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Library staff accounts can be retrieved via Patron Search and Check Out the same as a patron account.

You can easily retrieve a list of all library staff accounts at your library through the Patron Search.

. Set *Profile Group* to the Library Staff group applicable to your library type.
. Set the organizational unit to your library or system.
. Click *Search*.
+
image::images/admin/librarystaff/staff-account-4.png[]

Support recommends reviewing your staff accounts on a regular basis and 
xref:_closing_library_staff_accounts[closing accounts] for 
staff who no longer work at your library.


Closing Library Staff Accounts
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

link:https://youtu.be/d-EbdpforW8[*Sitka Training Video - Closing Library Staff Accounts (1:56)*]

When a staff member leaves the library, their staff access should be disabled in a timely manner.

.A local system administrator at your library needs to do the following:
. xref:_working_locations[Remove the working location(s)] from the account.
. xref:_removing_secondary_permission_group[Remove any secondary permission groups] granted to the account. 
+
[NOTE]
======
While local system administrators can only grant certain secondary permission groups, they can remove all of them.
======
+
. Change the main permission group to a patron permission group (or 
https://bc.libraries.coop/support/[contact Co-op Support] in the case of a local system
administrator).


If the former staff member will not be using the account as a personal borrowing account, a local system
administrator can use the http://docs.libraries.coop/sitka/_deleting_patron_accounts.html[patron deletion tool] to delete the account.

Additionally, if the staff member had access to any other library accounts, such as generic accounts,
those staff accounts must have their xref:_resetting_staff_account_password[passwords updated].

Recurring Reports
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

If the account being closed is currently used to run recurring reports for the library you have 
two options for handling those reports:

* If the account has only ever been used for staff access (no personal borrowing) you can update the name
 and details on the account (or contact https://bc.libraries.coop/support/[contact Co-op Support] in the 
 case of a local system administrator).  The new user MUST complete a 
 https://bc.libraries.coop/support/sitka/reporter-privacy-waiver/[reporter privacy waiver] to comply 
 with Sitka’s privacy policy.
+
*OR*
+
* If the account has been used as both a staff and personal borrowing account the reports will need 
to be moved to a new staff member.

Moving Reports to a New Staff Member
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

. Ensure that folder(s) the report templates currently reside in are 
xref:_sharing_folders[shared with your library]. Sharing them 
makes them visible to anyone at your library who has signed the https://bc.libraries.coop/support/sitka/reporter-privacy-waiver/full-reporter-privacy-waiver/[Full 
Reporter Privacy Waiver] and so has access to the Reporter. 
. The new staff member handling reports logs in and 
xref:_cloning_a_report_template[clones all the needed report templates] into their own 
folders, same as you would clone a report from the Sitka Templates folder.
. Any recurring reports running from the old account need to be 
xref:_deleting_report_definitions_and_output[cancelled] by going into the Reports folder 
for each report and deleting the report definition. 

[CAUTION]
=========
Deleting report definitions also deletes any output attached to the report so make sure to download any needed 
report output as Excel or CSV files and save them to your computer or network drive.

Report output that is more than 3 years old is deleted annually by Co-op Support.
=========
