Workstation Settings
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

There are a number of workstation settings that can be set by going to 
*Administration -> Workstation*. 

These settings apply to the logged in workstation. For the settings to take effect
you must log out and back into the workstation.

[NOTE]
======
With the exception of Sounds, these settings only apply to interfaces written 
in angular code, such as the staff catalogue. The settings will not apply to 
older interfaces, such as most of circulation.

Libraries can expect to see these settings apply to more interfaces in the future 
as the older parts of the staff client are re-written in the newer code.
======

Sounds
^^^^^^
(((Sounds)))
(((Disable Sounds)))

Sound is on by default in the Evergreen staff client. You can test the sounds and disable
them if desired.

.Test Sounds
. Click the buttons to test the staff client sounds.
+
image::images/admin/sounds-test-1.png[]
+
. Distinct sounds will be played for each event.
.. *Success*
.. *Info*
.. *Warning*
.. *Error*

.Disable Sounds
. Check the box for *Disable Sounds* to turn off the staff client sounds.
+
image::images/admin/sounds-test-2.png[]
+
. This will also disable the sound test buttons.

Prevent Links from Opening in New Tabs
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

. Check the box for *Prevent links from opening in new tabs* to force
all links to open in the same window.
+
image::images/admin/prevent-links-1.png[]
+
. Log out of the staff client.
. Clear your browser cache.  See xref:_clear_cache[].
. Log into the staff client.  Links will now only open in the same tab.

Include Full Library Names in Library Selector
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
(((Library Selector)))

link:https://youtu.be/pgDPRt3J-MM[*Sitka Snippet Video - Turning on Full Library Names (1:19)*]

. Check the box for *Include Full Library Names in Library Selector* to show the full name, 
as well as the short code, in library selectors in the staff client.
+
image::images/admin/library-selector-name-setting-1.png[]
+
. Log out of the staff client.
. Clear your browser cache.  See xref:_clear_cache[].
. Log into the staff client.  The full library name will now display in the library selector drop down.


Table Density
^^^^^^^^^^^^^

. From the drop down for *Table density* select Compact, Standard, and Wide.
+
image::images/admin/table-density-1.png[]
+
. Log out of the staff client.
. Clear your browser cache.  See xref:_clear_cache[].
. Log into the staff client.  The table density in newer interfaces will follow your
choice.
